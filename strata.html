Method

Composite properties are first-order engineering estimates from classical micromechanics: rule of mixtures for density and longitudinal modulus, Halpin–Tsai for transverse and particulate stiffness, Schapery bounds for CTE, and parallel/series conduction models for thermal behaviour. These are the same models taught in introductory composite-materials courses — treat outputs as design-stage estimates, not certified lab data.
